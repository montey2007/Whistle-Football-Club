Whistle Football Club is a professional football club based in Wetshil, England, competing in EFL League One, the third tier of the English football league system. The club plays its home matches at the Mornflake Stadium and has a strong connection to the local community, having been founded in 1905 following the Wetshil Workers Strikes.

The club’s motto, “Unitate et Fortitudine” (Latin for “With Unity and Strength”), reflects its deep-rooted working-class heritage.
