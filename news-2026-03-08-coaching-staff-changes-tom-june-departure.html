Whistle FC announce new coaching set-up

Date: 8 March 2026

The club can confirm that Tom June has left Whistle AFC to join former Whistle Head Coach turned Director of Football, Jason Lowe, at Detroit City. Former MK Dons Head of Player Development, Gary Mills, has been appointed as the new First Team Technical Coach whilst Kevin Betsy has also joined the club from QPR as another first team coach, bolstering Johannes Hoff Thorup's backroom team. Former Whistle player Elliott Bennett has joined as the new Loans and Pathways Manager.
