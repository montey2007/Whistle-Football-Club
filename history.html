Club History

Early Years and Foundation

Whistle Football Club was founded in August 1905 in the aftermath of the Wetshil Workers Strikes. Workers protested against poor conditions at Lord Wetshil’s factories. The unrest led to the burning of Whistle Castle before Lord Wetshil eventually agreed to the workers’ demands and established Whistle FC to unite the community through football.

The club initially competed in the Wetshil League and quickly gained prominence. By 1910 they had entered the English Football League Second Division and within two years won the FA Cup in 1912 defeating West Bromwich Albion.

Early Success and League Challenges (1910–1939)

Whistle FC secured promotion to the Second Division in 1911. However their early years in league football were turbulent with frequent promotions and relegations. The club dropped to the Third Division in 1933 but bounced back immediately as champions. This pattern repeated in 1938 and 1939.

Post-War Struggles and Decline (1945–1966)

Following World War II the club struggled to maintain league status and were relegated to the Third Division again in 1953. They returned to the Second Division in 1955 but a poor run of seasons eventually saw them drop into the Fourth Division by 1966.

Resurgence and Stability (1966–1998)

Whistle rebuilt in the late 1960s, securing two promotions in three years. From 1981 they spent sixteen consecutive seasons in the second tier before finally earning promotion to the Premier League in 1998.

Premier League and Financial Collapse (1998–2011)

Their Premier League stay lasted only one season before relegation. Continued financial problems eventually led to bankruptcy in June 2011 when the Wetshil family owners collapsed financially, forcing the club out of the Football League.

Rebirth and the Road Back (2012–2023)

Businessman Oliver Wilkinson purchased the club in February 2012 and stabilised its finances. Starting again in the Wetshil League, Whistle FC achieved eight promotions in ten years including promotions through the National League system before returning to the Football League in 2022.

  • 2014 - Wetshil Football League (Champions)
  • 2015 – North West Counties League Division One North (Champions)
  • 2016 – North West Counties League Premier Division (Promoted)
  • 2017 – Northern Premier League Division 1 (Promoted)
  • 2019 – Northern Premier League Premier Division (Promoted)
  • 2021 – National League North (Promoted)
  • 2022 – National League (Promoted)
  • 2024 – League Two (Promoted)

Return to League One and Future Aspirations (2024–Present)

After earning promotion to League One in 2024, the club underwent a managerial transition. Jason Lowe stepped down as head coach after 11 years to become Director of Football, with former player James Altwich appointed as his replacement. However, Altwich resigned due to illness, and his assistant Tom June led the club to promotion. In June 2024, Ian Foster was appointed as the new head coach, tasked with continuing Whistle FC’s remarkable journey back toward the upper tiers of English football. However disaster struck in February 2025 when the club was placed under investigation by the EFL for matters surrounding their sponsorship deal with Integral Spor. Ian Foster was sacked and replaced by Paul Lambert. In July 2025 the club were cleared of any wrongdoing. In summer 2025, Jason Lowe resigned as Director of Football. Marc Lettau was appointed Technical Director and Richard Montague was brought in as Sporting Director in a shake-up of the club’s management. Montague fell out with, and sacked, Paul Lambert in October 2025 and Johannes Hoff Thorup was appointed as his successor having bought into the Whistle Project.
