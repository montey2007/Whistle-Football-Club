Chambers Commits

📅 8 March 2026 | Club Announcement

We are delighted to announce that 18-year-old Rowan Chambers has signed his first professional contract which will keep him at the club until June 2028!

The England U18 international has made 10 senior appearances so far this season, including scoring his first senior goal in the Wetshil and Dattylandshire Cup.

Upon signing his first pro deal, Rowan explained what it meant to him:

"It’s an unbelievable feeling. Growing up, it’s every boy’s dream to become a professional footballer and for that to happen feels unbelievable. Growing up in Whistle and living here my whole life, to sign my first professional contract for this club makes it even more special."

Here's to a big two years Ro!
