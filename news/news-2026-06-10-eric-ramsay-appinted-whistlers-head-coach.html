Whistle FC Appoint Eric Ramsay as Head Coach

📅 10 June 2026 | Club News

Whistle Football Club is delighted to confirm the appointment of Eric Ramsay as the Club’s new Men's First Team Head Coach.

Ramsay joins following a spell in the Championship and brings a strong reputation as one of the most promising young coaches in British football.

The 34-year-old has previously held coaching roles at the highest level and now takes on his next challenge at the Vitality Stadium as the Whistlers continue their progression.

Club owner Oliver Wilkinson said:

“Eric is an outstanding young coach with clear ideas and a strong understanding of the modern game. We believe he is the right fit to build on the foundations already in place and continue moving the Club forward.”

Ramsay added:

“I’m delighted to be joining the Club. There is a clear vision here and a strong platform to build from. I’m excited to get to work and help take the Club to the next level.”

Ramsay will take charge of first-team affairs over the coming days with players set to return for pre-season next week.
