Whistle FC 2025/26 End of Season Award Winners

📅 3 May 2026 | Club News

Whistle Football Club are delighted to confirm the winners of the 2025/26 End of Season Awards, recognising the outstanding contributions across a memorable campaign.

The Whistlers enjoyed a strong season, finishing seventh in EFL League One and lifting the Vertu Trophy, with several players delivering exceptional performances throughout the year.

The headline award of the evening, Player of the Year, was awarded to Emmanuel Dennis following a superb campaign in front of goal.

“Emmanuel has been outstanding for us this season. His goals, his movement and his experience have made a huge difference to the team.

“He has led the line brilliantly and fully deserves this recognition.”

The Players’ Player of the Year award, voted for by the squad, was presented to Lewis Baker, whose leadership and consistency in midfield proved vital throughout the campaign.

“Lewis sets the standards every day. His experience, quality on the ball and influence in big moments have been key for us.

“He’s someone the players trust and rely on.”

The Young Player of the Year award went to Zac Williams, who impressed with a series of composed and consistent performances in defence.

“Zac has shown maturity beyond his years. He has stepped up this season and proved he can perform at this level consistently.

“This is just the beginning for him.”

The Junior Whistlers Player of the Year, voted for by the club’s young supporters, was awarded to Kevin Csoboth after an exciting second half of the season.

“Kevin brought energy, creativity and excitement to the team. He’s a player the young supporters really connect with.

“You can feel the buzz when he’s on the pitch.”

The Goal of the Season award was won by Paolo Sciortino for his outstanding strike against Peterborough United.

“It was a top-level goal. The technique and composure in that moment were exceptional.

“It’s exactly the kind of quality that can change games.”

The club would like to congratulate all winners and thank every player, member of staff and supporter for their contribution to the 2025/26 season.

Full List of Winners:
Player of the Year – Emmanuel Dennis
Players’ Player of the Year – Lewis Baker
Young Player of the Year – Zac Williams
Junior Rovers Player of the Year – Kevin Csoboth
Goal of the Season – Paolo Sciortino (vs Peterborough United)
