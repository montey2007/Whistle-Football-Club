Whistle FC Reveal 2026/27 Squad Numbers

📅 27 July 2026 | Club News

Whistle Football Club can today confirm the club's official squad numbers for the 2026/27 season.

New signings Ellery Balcombe, Kaelan Casey and Alex Sola will wear the numbers 1, 6 and 20.

"A squad number becomes part of a player's identity. Every shirt carries responsibility and represents the standards we expect at Whistle Football Club. We have assembled a group with quality, character and ambition, and we are excited to see these players represent our supporters throughout the season."

— Martin Thrush, CEO

The club's talented Under-21 players have also received their squad numbers as they continue their development and push for opportunities with the first team throughout the campaign.

"Every player, regardless of age or experience, has an important role to play. The young players have earned their place within the squad and now have another target to work towards as they continue their progression."

— Christian Preußer
