Welcome to Whistle, Conor Coady!

📅 2 September 2026 | Club News

Whistle Football Club are delighted to announce the signing of experienced defender Conor Coady.

The 33-year-old centre-back joins having signed a one-year deal as a free agent following his departure from Wrexham at the end of last month. Coady brings a wealth of experience to the club, having represented the likes of Liverpool, Huddersfield Town, Wolverhampton Wanderers, Everton, Leicester City and Wrexham during his career.

A former England international, Coady has earned 10 senior international caps and adds significant leadership and experience to the Whistle squad.

"I'm delighted to be joining Whistle Football Club. It's an exciting opportunity and one I'm really looking forward to. I've had some great conversations with the manager and the club about where they want to go, and I wanted to be part of that journey. I'll give everything I have for the shirt and can't wait to get started."

— Conor Coady

Head Coach Christian Preuber added:

"Conor is a fantastic addition to our squad. His experience, leadership and understanding of the game will be hugely valuable to us. He's played at the highest levels of English football and we believe he can make a big contribution both on and off the pitch."

— Christian Preuber

Everyone at Whistle Football Club would like to welcome Conor to the club and wishes him every success in a Whistle shirt.
