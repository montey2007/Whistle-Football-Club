Whistle FC Confirm 2026/27 Squad Numbers

📅 2 September 2026 | Club News

Whistle Football Club can today confirm the club's updated squad numbers for the remainder of the 2026/27 season.

Following a busy end to the summer transfer window, several changes have been made to the squad, including new arrivals and the departure of a number of first-team players.

New signing Tiago Codinha will wear the number 7, while Hasip Korkmazyürek takes the number 14. Donnell McNeilly has been allocated the number 21 following his arrival on loan.

The club has also allocated numbers to a number of its young players as they continue their development within the Whistle first-team setup.
