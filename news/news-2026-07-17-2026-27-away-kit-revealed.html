Whistle FC Reveal 2026/27 Away Kit

📅 17 July 2026 | Club News

Whistle Football Club are delighted to unveil the club's new Away Kit for the 2026/27 season.

Featuring a striking royal blue design with subtle black pinstripes and detailing, the new strip combines a modern appearance with the club's ambition as preparations continue for the upcoming campaign.

The kit also showcases the continued support of the club's valued partners, with EG Group remaining as principal sponsor alongside Glassvein Logistics, Wilkinson Trains, Wilko Air and Appin.

“We wanted our away kit to reflect the professionalism and ambition that continues to drive this football club forward. The design is modern, distinctive and one that we are proud to see represent Whistle FC throughout the season.”

— Oliver Wilkinson, Owner & Chairman

The launch comes ahead of Whistle FC's visit to Doncaster Rovers on Saturday, where supporters will get their first opportunity to see the new away kit worn in match action. The fixture represents another valuable test for Christian Preußer's side as preparations continue for the 2026/27 campaign.

Crafted using lightweight performance materials, the shirt has been designed to provide maximum comfort and freedom of movement while maintaining the high standards expected across all Whistle FC apparel.

The 2026/27 Away Kit is available now through the Whistle FC Club Shop and will make its on-pitch debut against Tranmere Rovers on Saturday.
