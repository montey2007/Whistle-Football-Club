Whistle Complete Deadline Day Double Signing!

📅 1 September 2026 | Club News

Whistle Football Club are delighted to announce a double signing on Deadline Day, with Tiago Codinha joining the club on loan and Hasip Korkmazyürek putting pen to paper on a two-year contract.

The two arrivals strengthen Christian Preuber's squad, with both players bringing versatility and quality to the midfield.

Tiago Codinha joins on loan

Attacking midfielder Tiago Codinha joins Whistle on a season-long loan from FC Volendam. The 21-year-old, who can also operate as a centre-forward, arrives with experience from Dutch youth and senior football.

Born in Hilversum and a Canadian international at U20 level, Codinha has developed through the academies of FC Utrecht and FC Twente before joining FC Volendam U21. He has also represented Canada U20, making two appearances for his country.

"I'm really excited to join Whistle. It's a great opportunity for me to play football, develop my game and help the team achieve its goals. I can't wait to get started and meet the supporters." — Tiago Codinha

Hasip Korkmazyürek signs two-year deal

Whistle have also secured the permanent signing of Hasip Korkmazyürek, who has signed a two-year contract with the club.

The 23-year-old central midfielder joins Whistle after spells in the Netherlands and Türkiye. Comfortable in central midfield as well as in attacking and defensive midfield roles, Korkmazyürek adds further versatility and experience to the squad.

Born in Berlin and a Turkish citizen, Korkmazyürek began his youth career in Germany before developing with Hansa Rostock and Istanbulspor. He most recently played for Isparta 32 Spor after previously being part of the Fortuna Sittard setup.

"I'm delighted to sign for Whistle Football Club. The ambition of the club really impressed me and I'm looking forward to being part of the team for the next two years. I can't wait to get started, work with the manager and my new teammates, and give everything for the club." — Hasip Korkmazyürek

Everyone at Whistle Football Club would like to welcome Tiago and Hasip to the club and wishes them every success in a Whistle shirt.

Welcome to Whistle, Tiago and Hasip!
