Whistle FC Confirm Pre-Season Schedule Ahead of 2027 Campaign

📅 10 June 2026 | Club News

Whistle Football Club can confirm our full pre-season schedule ahead of the 2026/27 campaign. Preparations will see the squad return to training on Monday 22 June, before a series of fixtures both in England and abroad as the team builds towards the new season.

The Whistlers will begin with a behind-closed-doors fixture at the training ground before travelling to face local and EFL opposition, followed by a warm-weather training camp in Spain.

  • 3 July – Closed-door friendly at training ground
  • 11 July – vs Gateshead (A)
  • 18 July – vs Doncaster Rovers (A)
  • 22 July – vs CD Eldense (Marbella Football Centre)
  • 26 July – vs Portsmouth (Marbella Football Centre)
  • 1 August – vs Norwich City (H)

The overseas training camp will take place between 20–27 July, providing an opportunity for intensive preparation and team bonding in a competitive environment. Fixtures against Spanish side CD Eldense and English opposition Portsmouth will form key parts of the camp schedule.

The final pre-season fixture will see Whistle FC host Norwich City at the Vitality Stadium on 1 August. The match will serve as the club’s final preparation ahead of the opening competitive fixtures of the season.

Competitive action begins shortly after, with the club entering cup competition between 7–9 August, before the league campaign gets underway the following weekend.

Supporters are encouraged to attend the home fixture against Norwich City and follow the club’s official channels for coverage throughout pre-season, including behind-the-scenes content from the training camp.
