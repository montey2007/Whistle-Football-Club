Whistle FC Confirm Pre-Season Schedule Ahead of 2026 Campaign

📅 8 May 2026 | Club News

Whistle Football Club can confirm our full pre-season schedule ahead of the 2026/27 campaign. Preparations will see the squad return to training on Monday 15 June, before a series of fixtures both in England and abroad as the team builds towards the new season.

The Whistlers will begin with fixtures against local opposition before heading abroad for a warm-weather training camp in Spain.

  • 11 July – vs Gateshead (A)
  • 18 July – vs Tranmere Rovers (A)
  • 22 July – vs CD Eldense (Marbella Football Centre)
  • 26 July – vs Portsmouth (Marbella Football Centre)
