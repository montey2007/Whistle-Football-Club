Club Statement: Johannes Hoff Thorup

📅 13 May 2026 | Club News

Whistle Football Club can confirm that Head Coach Johannes Hoff Thorup has departed the Club by mutual consent following discussions regarding the Club’s long-term direction.

Johannes joined the Club in June 2025 and oversaw an important season for Whistle FC, guiding the team to a seventh-place finish in EFL League One and lifting the Vertu Trophy at Wembley Stadium.

Following constructive discussions between all parties, it was agreed that now is the right time for the Club and Johannes to move in different directions.

Assistant Head Coach Glenn Riddersholm and First Team Coach Joe Schulberg will also depart the Club alongside Johannes.

Everybody at Whistle Football Club thanks Johannes and his staff for their professionalism and contribution to the Club and wishes them every success in the future.

The process of appointing a new Head Coach is now underway and further updates will be provided in due course.
