Whistle FC Welcome New Academy Scholars

📅 18 May 2026 | Academy News

Whistle Football Club are delighted to confirm the latest intake of academy scholars ahead of the 2026/27 season.

As the Club continues to strengthen its long-term player development pathway, a new group of talented young players will officially join the academy structure at the Vitality Stadium this summer.

Ten first-year scholars have signed scholarship agreements and will now begin their full-time development programme with the Club ahead of the new campaign.

Goalkeeper Ethan Slater, defenders Mason Crowther, Freddie Vaughan and Dylan Reeves, midfielders Noah Kent, Jacob Mullen and Callum Tate, alongside forwards Rio Saunders, Jamie Fletcher and Lucas Byrne, all join the Whistle FC academy scholarship programme.

The new intake brings together a strong blend of technical quality, athleticism and character, with several players having already impressed within the Club’s youth setup.

The Club can also confirm a number of new additions to the Under-21s group, as Whistle FC continue to build depth across the academy structure.

Kieran Doyle, Alfie Mercer, Milan Kovacevic, Tyler Bennett, Reece Dunne, Leo Hart, Kian Walters, Jayden Foster and Ben Redmond will all form part of the Club’s Under-21s squad for the 2026/27 season.

Academy staff and players recently gathered at the Vitality Stadium for the Club’s annual induction evening, where the scholars and their families were welcomed to the next stage of their footballing journeys.

Over the coming seasons, the young Whistlers will aim to establish themselves within the academy setup and continue progressing towards first-team football.

Introducing the Class of 2026

First-Year Scholars:
Ethan Slater, Mason Crowther, Freddie Vaughan, Dylan Reeves, Noah Kent, Jacob Mullen, Callum Tate, Rio Saunders, Jamie Fletcher and Lucas Byrne.

Everyone at Whistle Football Club congratulates the new scholars and wishes them every success during their academy journeys.
