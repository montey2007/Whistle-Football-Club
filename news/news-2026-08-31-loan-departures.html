Three Whistle Youngsters Head Out on Loan

📅 31 August 2026 | Club News

Whistle Football Club can confirm that three of the club's promising young players will spend the upcoming season out on loan, with Kacper Jenkins, Rowan Chambers and Luca Elliot all departing temporarily to gain valuable first-team experience.

The trio will join clubs across the English football pyramid, with each player looking to continue their development by gaining regular competitive football away from Whistle.

Kacper Jenkins joins Fleetwood Town

Kacper Jenkins will spend the season on loan at Fleetwood Town. The 18-year-old can operate as a right wing-back or striker and will be looking to gain valuable senior experience during his spell away from Whistle.

Jenkins remains under contract with Whistle until June 2027, and the loan move represents an important opportunity for the youngster to continue his development in a competitive first-team environment.

Rowan Chambers joins Newport County

Rowan Chambers has joined Newport County on loan. The 18-year-old is capable of playing as a left wing-back or striker and will now have the opportunity to experience regular senior football.

Chambers is contracted to Whistle until June 2028, and the club believes this loan spell will provide valuable experience as he continues his development.

Luca Elliot joins Cheltenham Town

Luca Elliot will spend the season on loan at Cheltenham Town. The 18-year-old defensive midfielder joins the League Two side as he looks to take the next step in his development.

Elliot is under contract with Whistle until June 2027 and will now have the opportunity to gain valuable first-team experience while continuing his progression.

"These loan moves are a really important part of the development of Kacper, Rowan and Luca. All three have huge potential, and getting regular competitive football will be invaluable for them. We'll be keeping a close eye on their progress and look forward to seeing them return to Whistle stronger and more experienced." — Richard Montague, Director of Football

Everyone at Whistle Football Club wishes Kacper, Rowan and Luca every success during their loan spells. We look forward to following their progress throughout the season.
