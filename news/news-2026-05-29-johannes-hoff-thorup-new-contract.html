Hoff Thorup Commits!

📅 28 May 2026 | Club News

Whistle Football Club is delighted to confirm that Head Coach Johannes Hoff Thorup has signed a new long-term contract with the Club.

The agreement follows a period of significant external interest in Johannes after an impressive first season at the Vitality Stadium, during which Whistle FC achieved a seventh-place finish in EFL League One and lifted the Vertu Trophy at Wembley Stadium.

Johannes joined the Club in June 2025 and has played an important role in the continued development of Whistle FC both on and off the pitch, helping to further establish the Club’s progressive footballing identity and high-performance culture.

The new agreement reflects the Club’s commitment to continuity, stability and long-term progression as preparations continue for the 2026/27 campaign.

Chairman Oliver Wilkinson said:

“Johannes has had a significant impact since arriving at Whistle FC and we are delighted that he has committed his future to the Club. There was understandable interest from elsewhere, but our focus throughout has been on building a structure that allows talented people to succeed here.”

Johannes Hoff Thorup said:

“I am very happy to continue at Whistle FC. This is a Club with ambition, strong people and a clear football vision. We made good progress last season, but there is still a lot of work ahead of us and I am excited for what comes next.”

The Club will now continue preparations for the 2026/27 season, with further football updates to follow in due course.
