Whistle FC announce new coaching set-up

📅 8 March 2026 | Club Announcement

The club can confirm that Tom June has left Whistle AFC to join former Whistle Head Coach turned Director of Football, Jason Lowe, at Detroit City.

Former MK Dons Head of Player Development Gary Mills has been appointed as the new First Team Technical Coach.

Kevin Betsy has also joined the club from QPR as another first team coach, strengthening Johannes Hoff Thorup's backroom team.

Former Whistle player Elliott Bennett has joined the club as the new Loans and Pathways Manager.
