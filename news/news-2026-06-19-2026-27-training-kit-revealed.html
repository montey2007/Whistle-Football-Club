Whistle FC Reveal New 2026/27 Training Collection

📅 19 June 2026 | Club News

Whistle Football Club are delighted to unveil the club's brand-new training collection for the 2026/27 season ahead of the players' return for pre-season training on Monday.

Designed to combine performance, comfort, and the club's modern identity, the new collection will be worn by all first-team players and coaching staff throughout pre-season and the upcoming campaign.

“As we continue building for the future, it is important that every aspect of the club reflects our ambition and identity. The new training collection looks fantastic and will give our players and staff high-quality performance wear as we begin preparations for the new season.”

— Oliver Wilkinson, Owner & Chairman

The squad will report back to the Barracks training centre on Monday morning as preparations begin for what promises to be an exciting 2026/27 campaign under new Head Coach Christian Preußer.
