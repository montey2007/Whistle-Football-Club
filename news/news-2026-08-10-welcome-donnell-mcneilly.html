Welcome to Whistle, Donnell McNeilly!

📅 10 August 2026 | Club News

Whistle Football Club are delighted to announce the signing of English forward Donnell McNeilly on loan from Premier League side Nottingham Forest.

The 20-year-old centre-forward joins the club after impressing at youth level, bringing valuable experience from Premier League 2 and senior football to Christian Preuber's squad.

McNeilly spent last term on loan at Wycombe Wanderers and arrives at Whistle with 113 senior and youth appearances to his name, including 56 goals and 12 assists across a range of competitions.

"I'm really excited to join Whistle Football Club and get started. It's a great opportunity for me to play senior football, develop my game and contribute to the team. I've spoken with the manager and the staff and I'm really impressed by the ambition here. I'll give everything for the club and the supporters and hopefully we can have a successful season together." — Donnell McNeilly

Everyone at Whistle Football Club would like to welcome Donnell to the club and wishes him every success in a Whistle shirt.
