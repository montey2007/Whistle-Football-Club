Wetshil and Dattylandshire Cup Quarter Final Draw

📅 9 March 2026 | Fixture Update

The club are pleased to announce we have drawn to face Southerd FC in the Wetshil and Dattylandshire Cup Quarter Finals.

The match will be played at The Mornflake Stadium on Tuesday 24th March 2026.

The full draw can be found below: Whistle FC vs Southerd FC Switleh United vs Tristy Alexandra Deebridge FC vs Minshead FC St Thaldine Rovers vs Dartley United

Ticket details will be announced in due course.
