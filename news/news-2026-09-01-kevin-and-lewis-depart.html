Whistle Announce Double Departure

📅 1 September 2026 | Club News

Whistle Football Club can confirm the departures of Kevin Csoboth and Lewis Baker from the club.

Both players leave Whistle following their time with the club, bringing an end to their respective spells as the club continues to shape its squad under our new head coach.

Kevin Csoboth departs

Kevin Csoboth leaves Whistle after twelve months at the club to join Turkish outfit Genclerbirligi Ankara for an undisclosed fee. The Hungarian attacker brought 6 goals and 7 assists across 24 appearances last term but departs having made just 2 appearances so far in 2026/27.

Whistle Football Club would like to thank Kevin for his commitment and professionalism during his time with the club and wish him every success for the next chapter of his career.

Lewis Baker departs

Lewis Baker also departs Whistle, bringing his two-year spell with the club to an end as he joins Bursaspor for an undisclosed fee. The experienced central midfielder has been an important presence in the squad, providing 2 goals and 6 assists in his 26 appearances for the club.

Everyone at Whistle Football Club would like to thank Lewis for his contribution to the club and wish him all the very best for the future.

"We would like to thank both Kevin and Lewis for everything they have given to Whistle Football Club. They have both conducted themselves brilliantly during their time here, and we wish them every success in their future careers." — Richard Montague, Director of Football

Everyone at Whistle Football Club would like to thank Kevin and Lewis for their efforts and wish them every success for the future.
