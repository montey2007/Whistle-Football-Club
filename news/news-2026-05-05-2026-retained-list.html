Whistle FC Confirm Retained List

📅 5 May 2026 | Club News

Whistle Football Club can confirm its retained list following the conclusion of the 2025/26 season.

After a campaign which saw the Club finish seventh in EFL League One and lift the Vertu Trophy, plans are already well underway ahead of the 2026/27 season as the Whistlers continue their progression.

The Club can confirm that the option to make Kevin Csoboth’s move permanent has been activated following a successful loan spell from St. Gallen.

The winger made a significant impact during the second half of the season and will now remain at the Vitality Stadium as part of Johannes Hoff Thorup’s squad moving forward.

The club are also delighted to announce that new contracts have been signed by Nathaniel Pratt, Luca Elliot, Darrel Gaines and Kacper Jenkins, extending their stays at the Vitality Stadium by twelve months.

The Club remains committed to rewarding performance, experience and developing young talent from within its academy structure.

Tomas Slavadaski, Craig Harris, Jonathan Bond, Steven Trevino, Jonah Thompson, Ilgi Rahmanzai, Xavier Harrison, Caleb Tanner and Adam Khan will leave the Club upon the expiration of their contracts this summer.

The Club would like to thank all players for their professionalism and contribution during their time at Whistle FC.

The following players will return to their parent clubs following the conclusion of their loan spells: Michael Olakigbe, Dominic Iorfa, Callum Osmand, Theo De Percin and Samuel Rak-Sakyi.

Everyone at Whistle FC thanks them for their efforts and wishes them every success in the future.
