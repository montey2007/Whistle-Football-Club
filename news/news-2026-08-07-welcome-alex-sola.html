Welcome to Whistle, Alex Sola!

📅 7 August 2026 | Club News

Whistle Football Club are delighted to announce the signing of versatile Angolan midfielder Alex Sola from Portuguese side CF Estrela Amadora.

The 22-year-old has penned a long-term contract through to the summer of 2030 and becomes our third summer arrival under new Head Coach, Christian Preuber.

Alex will be available to feature tomorrow in our Carabao Cup First Round tie at Gillingham, subject to international clearance.

"I'm incredibly excited to join Whistle Football Club. From my first conversations with the manager and the staff, I could see the ambition the club has and the direction it's heading. I want to work hard every day, help the team achieve its goals, and give everything for the supporters. I can't wait to get started and hopefully make a positive impact straight away."

— Alex Sola

Everyone at Whistle Football Club would like to welcome Alex to the club and wishes him every success in a Whistle shirt.
