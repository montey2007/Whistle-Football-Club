Whistle FC Announce New Partnership with EG Group

📅 30 April 2026 | Club News

Whistle Football Club are delighted to announce a new partnership with EG Group, one of the world’s leading independent convenience retailers.

Founded locally in 2001, EG Group has grown from a single UK forecourt into a globally recognised business with a major presence across grocery and merchandise, foodservice and fuel retail. Despite its international growth, the company remains proud of its Lancashire roots, making this partnership especially significant for the Club and the local community.

As part of the agreement, EG Group will become the Club’s new front-of-shirt sponsor from the start of the 2026/27 season, replacing Glassvein Logistics, who have supported the Club with distinction in recent campaigns. Whistle FC would like to place on record its sincere thanks to Glassvein Logistics for their valued contribution during an important period of growth.

The partnership represents another important step forward for Whistle FC as the club continues to grow both on and off the pitch. EG Group’s support will help strengthen the club’s commercial platform while supporting our wider ambitions within the local area.

Club owner Oliver Wilkinson said:

“We are proud to welcome EG Group as a partner of Whistle Football Club. Their story is one of ambition, growth and hard work, values that closely match what we are building here at the football club.

“To be partnering with a company that was founded locally makes this even more special, as we continue to strengthen our ties within the region.”

CEO Martin Thrush added:

“This is a significant partnership for the club. EG Group are a major name with a strong reputation, and we are excited about working together as Whistle FC continues to move forward.

“Having their branding on the front of our shirts represents a huge milestone and reflects the progress being made across the club.”

Co-founders of EG Group, Mohsin Issa and Zuber Issa, said:

“We are delighted to partner with Whistle Football Club at such an exciting time in its development. The club’s ambition, community focus and forward-thinking approach align closely with our own values at EG Group, and we look forward to building a strong and successful relationship together.”

Whistle FC would like to thank EG Group for their support and looks forward to a successful partnership.
