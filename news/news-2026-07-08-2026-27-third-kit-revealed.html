Whistle FC Unveil Striking 2026/27 Third Kit

📅 8 July 2026 | Club News

Whistle Football Club are delighted to unveil the club's new Third Kit for the 2026/27 season.

Featuring a bold pink design with black trim and a detailed illustration of the historic Whistle Castle across the lower half of the shirt, the new strip celebrates both the club's modern ambitions and its connection to the local community.

The kit also showcases the continued support of the club's valued partners, with EG Group remaining as principal sponsor alongside Glassvein Logistics, Wilkinson Trains, Wilko Air and Appin.

“We wanted our third kit to be something unique and memorable. The design celebrates our identity, our history and the community we represent. It is a shirt that stands out and one that we are incredibly proud to see our players wear.”

— Oliver Wilkinson, Owner & Chairman

The launch comes just days before Christian Preußer's side travel to Gateshead for their opening pre-season fixture on Saturday. Supporters making the trip to the Gateshead International Stadium will get their first opportunity to see the new third kit in action as the squad continues preparations for the season ahead.

Crafted using lightweight performance materials, the shirt has been designed to provide maximum comfort and freedom of movement while maintaining the high standards expected across all Whistle FC apparel.

The 2026/27 Third Kit is available now through the Whistle FC Club Shop.
