2026/27 Cup Dates Confirmed

📅 30 June 2026 | Club News

Whistle Football Club can confirm its cup schedule for the 2026/27 season following the release of the fixture dates for the domestic cup competitions.

The Whistlers will compete in the Carabao Cup, Emirates FA Cup, EFL Trophy and the Wetshil and Dattylandshire Cup during the campaign, while the club has taken the decision not to enter the Wetshil Cup this season.

Carabao Cup

Whistle FC have been drawn away to Gillingham in the First Round, with the tie set to be played over the weekend of 7–9 August 2026.

Round Dates

  • First Round – Weekend of 7–9 August 2026 (Away to Gillingham)
  • Second Round – Week commencing 24 August 2026
  • Third Round – Weeks commencing 7 & 14 September 2026
  • Fourth Round – Week commencing 26 October 2026
  • Fifth Round – Week commencing 14 December 2026
  • Semi-Final First Leg – Week commencing 11 January 2027
  • Semi-Final Second Leg – Week commencing 1 February 2027
  • Final – Sunday 21 March 2027

EFL Trophy

Whistle FC have been drawn in a group alongside Peterborough United, Colchester United and Ipswich Town Under-21s.

Group stage fixture dates will be confirmed in due course.

Round Dates

  • Group Stage – From early September 2026
  • Round of 32 – Week commencing 7 December 2026
  • Round of 16 – Week commencing 11 January 2027
  • Quarter-Finals – Week commencing 15 February 2027
  • Semi-Finals – Week commencing 8 March 2027
  • Final – Sunday 11 April 2027

Emirates FA Cup

Round Dates

  • First Round Proper – Saturday 7 November 2026
  • Second Round Proper – Saturday 5 December 2026
  • Third Round Proper – Saturday 9 January 2027
  • Fourth Round Proper – Saturday 13 February 2027
  • Fifth Round Proper – Saturday 6 March 2027
  • Quarter-Final – Saturday 3 April 2027
  • Semi-Final – Saturday 24 April 2027
  • Final – Saturday 22 May 2027

Wetshil & Dattylandshire Cup

Round Dates

  • Preliminary Round – 22 December 2026
  • First Round – 26 January 2027
  • Second Round – 23 February 2027
  • Quarter-Final – 16 March 2027
  • Semi-Final – 20 April 2027
  • Final – 2 May 2027
"Cup competitions always provide special occasions throughout the season, and we're looking forward to testing ourselves across three major competitions while continuing our focus on the league campaign."

— Christian Preußer

Individual fixture dates, kick-off times and ticket information will be announced once confirmed by the relevant competition organisers.

Supporters are encouraged to keep an eye on the club's official channels for the latest updates throughout the season.
