Injury Update: Emmanuel Dennis and Lucas Bergstrom

📅 17 March 2026 | Injury News

Ahead of our clash against Exeter City later, Johannes Hoff Thorup has revealed that goalkeeper Lucas Bergstrom will return to the matchday squad, boosting JHT's depth.

Johannes also confirmed that striker Emmanuel Dennis has been ruled out for the remainder of the season, having sustained a lower leg injury at Wycombe on Saturday.

Johannes couldn't underestimate how big a loss this is:

"Denno has been incredible this season, and offers such a threat whether that's on the right, or in the more central role. He's going to be a huge loss in our play-off push, but we will support him and look forward to his return in time for the new campaign, hopefully in the Championship."

Johannes also provided an update on the other quintet currently missing:

"Lewis [Baker] is back on grass now, so is Kyriakos [Neofytou]. They are both progressing well. Paolo [Sciortino] will play for the U21s this weekend and could be back for the cup next week. The squad is in a good place as we approach the run-in."

Get down to support the lads tonight, it's going to be a huge game!
