Spain Travelling Squad Confirmed

📅 19 July 2026 | First Team News

Whistle Football Club can confirm the 26-player travelling squad for the Club's pre-season training camp in Spain.

The squad will travel to Spain this week for an intensive programme of warm-weather training as preparations continue ahead of the 2026/27 campaign.

During the camp, the Whistlers will take on Spanish Segunda División side CD Eldense before concluding the trip with a friendly against fellow English outfit Portsmouth.

The week-long camp will provide the coaching staff with another valuable opportunity to continue implementing the new manager's playing style, while further improving the squad's fitness and tactical understanding ahead of the opening league fixture.

Several academy players have been included in the travelling party following impressive performances during the opening stages of pre-season. Goalkeeper Youseff Gray, defenders Juan Nolan and Nile Tenter, together with midfielder Tyler Bennett, will all travel with the first-team squad.

Following the opening two pre-season fixtures, the camp in Spain will also give a number of players further opportunities to impress as competition for places continues across the squad.

Travelling Squad

Goalkeepers:
Ellery Balcombe, Nathan Shepperd, Youseff Gray.

Defenders:
Emmanuel van de Blaak, Zac Williams, Nathaniel Pratt, Darrel Gaines, Juan Nolan, Nile Tenter, Perry Ng, James Morris, Mario Tsaousis, Andres Lawson, Kevin Csoboth.

Midfielders:
Mark Zabukovnick, Lewis Baker, Callum Jones, Luca Elliot, Tyler Bennett, Boris van Schuppen, Paolo Sciortino, Kyriakos Neofytou.

Forwards:
Emmanuel Dennis, Max Watters, Oscar Petterson, Rowan Chambers.

The players will spend the week combining twice-daily training sessions with tactical meetings and recovery work before facing CD Eldense and Portsmouth in two important pre-season tests.

The fixtures are expected to provide the strongest examinations of pre-season so far, with both opponents offering different tactical challenges as the Whistlers continue building towards the start of the 2026/27 season.
