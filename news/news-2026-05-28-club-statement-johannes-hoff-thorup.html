Club Statement: Johannes Hoff Thorup

📅 28 May 2026 | Club News

Whistle Football Club can confirm that Head Coach Johannes Hoff Thorup has departed the Club to take up a new opportunity in European football.

Thorup leaves after a single season, having guided the Club to a seventh-place finish in EFL League One and lifting the Vertu Trophy.

The Club thanks Johannes for his contribution and professionalism, and wishes him every success in the next stage of his career.

Assistant Coaches Glenn Riddersholm and Joe Schulberg have also departed the Club.

The process to appoint a new Head Coach is now underway and further updates will be provided in due course.
