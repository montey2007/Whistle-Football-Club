Club Statement: Johannes Hoff Thorup

📅 28 May 2026 | Club News

Whistle Football Club can confirm that Head Coach Johannes Hoff Thorup has departed the Club by mutual consent in order to pursue a new opportunity in European football.

Johannes joined the Club in June 2025 and played an important role in the continued development of Whistle FC both on and off the pitch.

During his time at the Vitality Stadium, the Club achieved a seventh-place finish in EFL League One, narrowly missing out on the play-off positions, while also lifting the Vertu Trophy at Wembley Stadium.

Beyond results, Johannes helped further establish the progressive footballing identity and high-performance culture that continues to drive the Club forward as part of its long-term vision.

Assistant Head Coach Glenn Riddersholm and First Team Coach Joe Schulberg will also depart the Club alongside Johannes.

Everybody at the Club wishes Johannes and his staff every success for the future.

The process of appointing a new Head Coach is now underway and the Club will provide further updates in due course.
