Whistle FC Complete the Signing of Kaelan Casey

📅 22 July 2026 | Club News

Whistle Football Club are delighted to announce the signing of promising central defender Kaelan Casey on a season-long loan from Championship side West Ham United.

The 21-year-old will travel to Spain immediately to link up with Christian Preußer's squad for the remainder of pre-season. Casey arrives for his third spell away from the London Stadium, having spent time on loan with Swansea City and Leyton Orient last term.

A composed ball-playing defender, Casey is highly regarded for his aerial ability, reading of the game and confidence in possession. Having progressed through West Ham United's academy, he now arrives at Whistle looking to continue his development with regular first-team football.

"Kaelan is a talented young defender who we believe possesses all the right characteristics to become a top player. He's comfortable on the ball, defends aggressively and has a fantastic attitude. We're delighted he's chosen Whistle as the next step in his career."

— Richard Montague, Director of Football

Casey becomes the second exciting addition to Christian Preußer's squad as preparations continue for the 2026/27 season.

"I'm really excited to be here. As soon as I spoke to the manager and the Director of Football, I knew this was the right move for me. The ambition around the club is obvious, and I can't wait to meet the supporters, get started with the lads and help the team achieve something special this season."

— Kaelan Casey

Casey joins the squad with immediate effect and is expected to feature during the club's remaining pre-season fixtures before the start of the 2026/27 campaign.

Everyone at Whistle Football Club would like to welcome Kaelan to the club and wishes him every success in a Whistle shirt.
