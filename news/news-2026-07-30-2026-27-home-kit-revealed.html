Whistle FC Reveal 2026/27 Home Kit

📅 30 July 2026 | Club News

Whistle Football Club are delighted to unveil the club's official Home Kit for the 2026/27 season.

The new strip sees the return of Whistle FC's iconic red and black colours, combining the club's proud traditions with a modern design ahead of what promises to be an exciting campaign under Head Coach Christian Preußer.

Featuring bold vertical stripes, premium performance materials and the continued support of principal partner EG Group, the shirt represents the identity, ambition and unity that continue to drive the football club forward.

“The home shirt is always the most important kit a football club releases. It represents our supporters, our history and everything that makes Whistle Football Club special. We wanted a design that respects our traditions while looking confidently towards the future, and we are incredibly proud of the final result.”

— Oliver Wilkinson, Owner & Chairman

The launch comes just two days before Whistle FC welcome Norwich City to the Vitality Stadium for the club's final pre-season fixture before the start of the 2026/27 season.

Supporters attending Saturday's match will be the first to see the new home kit worn in competitive action as Christian Preußer's side complete their preparations for the new campaign.

Speaking ahead of the launch, Head Coach Christian Preußer highlighted the importance of the connection between the team and the supporters.

“The home shirt carries great meaning because it represents the bond between the players, the club and the supporters. We have worked extremely hard throughout pre-season and we are looking forward to wearing these colours in front of our fans for the first time this season.”

— Christian Preußer

Crafted using lightweight, high-performance materials, the new home kit has been designed to maximise comfort, durability and freedom of movement while maintaining the high standards expected across all Whistle FC apparel.

The launch completes Whistle FC's 2026/27 kit collection following the successful unveiling of the club's away and third kits earlier this summer.

The 2026/27 Home Kit is available now through the Whistle FC Club Shop and will make its on-pitch debut against Norwich City on Saturday.
