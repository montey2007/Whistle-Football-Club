Whistle FC Complete the Signing of Ellery Balcombe

📅 30 June 2026 | Club News

Whistle Football Club are delighted to announce the signing of goalkeeper Ellery Balcombe following the expiry of his contract with Premier League side Brentford.

The 26-year-old arrives at the Vitality Stadium bringing a wealth of experience despite his young age, having enjoyed loan spells across England, Denmark and Scotland throughout his career. A former England Under-21 international, Balcombe has built a reputation as an athletic, commanding goalkeeper with excellent distribution and strong shot-stopping ability.

Having developed through Brentford's academy, Balcombe gained valuable first-team experience with Boreham Wood, Viborg FF, Doncaster Rovers, Bromley, Burton Albion, Crawley Town, Bristol Rovers, St. Mirren and Motherwell. His performances in both League One and the Scottish Premiership have demonstrated his ability to perform consistently at a high level.

"Ellery is a goalkeeper we've admired for a long time. He possesses outstanding athleticism, great presence in his penalty area and the mentality we want throughout this football club. Despite already having excellent experience, we believe his best years are still ahead of him and we're delighted he's chosen Whistle FC for the next chapter of his career."

— Richard Montague, Director of Football

Balcombe becomes the first addition to Christian Preußer's squad ahead of the club's inaugural campaign and will immediately compete for the number one shirt as preparations continue for the 2026/27 season.

"I'm incredibly excited to join Whistle FC. After speaking with the manager and the chairman, it was clear how ambitious this project is and where the club wants to go. I can't wait to get started, meet my teammates and play in front of the supporters. My aim is to help the club achieve success from the very beginning."

— Ellery Balcombe

Balcombe officially joins the club on 1 July following the expiration of his Brentford contract and will link up with his new teammates immediately for the start of pre-season.

Everyone at Whistle Football Club would like to welcome Ellery to the Vitality Stadium and wishes him every success in a Whistle shirt.
