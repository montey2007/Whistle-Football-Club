Wembley awaits as Whistle FC prepare for EFL Trophy Final

📅 9 April 2026 | Match Preview

Whistle FC will head to Wembley Stadium this Sunday as Johannes Hoff Thorup’s side prepare for one of the biggest matches in the club’s modern history — the EFL Trophy Final against Stockport County.

After a remarkable campaign full of drama, resilience and unforgettable moments, the Reds now stand just one game away from silverware under the famous Wembley arch.

Supporters are expected to travel in huge numbers to back the team in what promises to be a special occasion for everyone connected with the club.

Whistle come into the final in strong form and full of confidence, with momentum continuing to build both in the league and cup competitions. The squad has shown character throughout the season, and Sunday presents the chance to turn an impressive campaign into a truly memorable one.

Head Coach Johannes Hoff Thorup has praised the togetherness within the group in the build-up to the match, with players and staff fully focused on the challenge ahead.

Speaking ahead of the final, Thorup said:

"These are the matches everyone wants to be involved in. Wembley is a special place, but our focus has to stay on the performance. We’ve earned the right to be here and now we want to finish the job."

Standing in Whistle’s way are a strong Stockport County side, who have also enjoyed an excellent season and will provide a stern test on the national stage.

The two teams have both shown quality and consistency throughout the campaign, setting up what promises to be a fiercely contested final between two ambitious sides.

For Whistle supporters, Sunday is more than just a football match — it is another landmark moment in the club’s rise and an opportunity to see their team compete for major silverware on the biggest domestic stage.

Everyone at the club is encouraging supporters to make Wembley a sea of red and bring the noise from start to finish.

Wembley. Sunday. One more push.
